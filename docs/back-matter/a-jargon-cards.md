Read each card as **short form → full form → memory clue**. These are <Highlight>common meanings in AI and data science</Highlight>; a few letters can mean different things in different contexts.

<JargonBoard />

### Same letters, different meaning

- **LR** often means *learning rate*, but can mean *linear regression* or *logistic regression*. This guide writes those as **LinReg** and **LogReg**.
- **ANN** can mean *Artificial Neural Network* or *Approximate Nearest Neighbor*. This guide writes **NN** for a neural network and **ANN search** for vector retrieval.
- **R** can mean the *R programming language* or *recall*. Always read the surrounding sentence.
