Definitions in this guide are simplified for revision. Use these textbooks, papers and official documentation for deeper study and implementation details.

1. Russell, S. J. and Norvig, P. *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*, 4th edition, Pearson, 2021.
2. Hastie, T., Tibshirani, R. and Friedman, J. *The Elements of Statistical Learning*, 2nd edition, Springer, 2009 (author-hosted book).
3. James, G., Witten, D., Hastie, T. and Tibshirani, R. *An Introduction to Statistical Learning*, 2nd edition, Springer, 2021.
4. Goodfellow, I., Bengio, Y. and Courville, A. *Deep Learning*, MIT Press, 2016.
5. Sutton, R. S. and Barto, A. G. *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction*, 2nd edition, MIT Press, 2018 (author-hosted book).
6. Vaswani, A. et al. “Attention Is All You Need,” 2017 (arXiv paper).
7. scikit-learn User Guide — preprocessing, model selection, metrics and estimator-specific behaviour.
8. NIST AI Risk Management Framework — responsible design and governance.
9. For vendor-specific material, the current official documentation for LangChain, LangGraph, LangSmith and MLflow; for the protocol, the Model Context Protocol specification.
