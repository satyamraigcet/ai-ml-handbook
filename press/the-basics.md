### A · The basics

What the fields are, before any algorithm.

<QAItem q="What is AI?">
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of creating systems that perform tasks associated with intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception and decision-making. An AI system can solve such tasks without processing information in the same way a human does.
</QAItem>
<QAItem q="What is ML (Machine Learning)?">
Machine Learning (ML) is a field of Artificial Intelligence that focuses on building models and algorithms that can <Highlight>learn from data</Highlight> and make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed. It involves techniques such as supervised learning, unsupervised learning, semi-supervised learning, and reinforcement learning.
</QAItem>
<QAItem q="What is NLP?">
Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a field of AI that focuses on enabling computers to understand, interpret and generate human language. It uses statistical methods and deep learning models to process and analyze text or speech data for tasks such as translation, sentiment analysis, chatbots, question answering and text summarization.
</QAItem>
<QAItem q="What is supervised learning?">
In supervised learning, the model is trained on <Highlight>labeled data</Highlight>, where the data contains input features and their corresponding output labels (target values). The model learns the <Highlight>mapping between input and output</Highlight> to make predictions on new data.

**Examples:** Linear Regression, Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, Random Forest, Support Vector Machines (SVM).
</QAItem>
<QAItem q="What is unsupervised learning?">
In unsupervised learning, the model is trained on unlabeled data. The goal is to find hidden patterns or structure in the data, such as grouping similar data points together.

**Examples:** K-means clustering, Hierarchical clustering, PCA (Principal Component Analysis), Association Rule Mining.
</QAItem>
